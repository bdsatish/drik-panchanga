    Lunar month

    Amānta (new moon → new moon) is usual in South India; Pūrṇimānta (full moon → full moon) in much of North India. Affects day māsa badges and PDF masa labels; festival dates stay on amānta rules.

    Ayanāṃśa

    Sidereal zero-point used for nakṣatra, yoga, and related fields. True Citra is the default.

    Day panchanga
